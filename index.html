    Straight answers

    Questions

    The rules below are the same ones the tool enforces. If the answer here and the tool ever disagree, email us and we will fix one of them.

    What is SurgeLab?

    SurgeLab is marketing on autopilot for small and mid-sized businesses. Its AI agents check how ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude find and describe your business, fix what they get wrong, and run your marketing every week: posts, reviews, your Google listing and a score out of 100 you can watch move. You approve. It runs.

    Is this a marketing service or an AI tool?

    A marketing team that happens to run on AI. It gets you found, keeps you posting, answers your reviews and fixes what is wrong, every week. You sign in, approve what it made, and get on with your day. No account manager, no retainer, no meetings.

    How much does SurgeLab cost?

    One plan, Front Door: £199 a month, or £2,149 a year, which is 10% off. Prices exclude VAT. Cancel any month. Video and AI marketing agents are optional add-ons priced inside the tool. Agencies pay by brand count and book a call for their rate.

    Which AI assistants does SurgeLab check?

    ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude. The full tool asks all four every week, live, the way a customer would. The free two-minute audit asks two of them.

    What counts as an image?

    Any finished picture SurgeLab generates for your brand: a product shot, a lifestyle image or a branded avatar picture. Your plan includes 100 a month and the count resets on your billing date. Photos you upload yourself never count, and neither do test runs.

    What is a video credit?

    One credit makes one finished video. If a video fails, the credit comes straight back. Unused credits carry over while you subscribe, up to two months' worth. Video is an add-on, bought inside the app.

    Can I cancel?

    Yes, any month. Open Billing in the tool and press Manage billing. Annual plans run to the end of the year you paid for. On request we hand you a copy of everything generated for you and then delete your data.

    Do prices include VAT?

    No. All prices shown exclude VAT, which is added at checkout where it applies.

    What happens to my data?

    Your brand data and generated content are kept while your account is active and deleted on request. We do not sell personal data, we do not run advertising cookies, and content sent to AI providers is used only to generate your content. Card details are handled by Stripe and never stored by SurgeLab. The full detail is in the privacy policy.

    Does anything publish without my approval?

    No. Finished posts land in a review screen where you approve, reject or regenerate each one. Full automation is a switch you turn on yourself, and you can turn it off again.
