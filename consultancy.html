Who this suits

Where it has worked

The pattern is the same in every case: a business with good people, a lot of accumulated information, and no obvious way to get AI into the middle of it without breaking what already works.

Family offices

Years of documents, correspondence and holdings sitting in places nobody can search properly. The work is making all of it answerable, without any of it leaving where it should be.

Recruitment

A database full of people and roles that only pays off if someone remembers who is in it. The work is matching, drafting and follow-up done in the background, with a human making every decision that counts.

SurgeLab itself

The clearest example. A team of eight AI specialists with a manager keeping them honest, running marketing for real businesses every week. Built from scratch, and the reason any of this advice is worth taking.

Who you are dealing with

Twelve years in tech,
then this

Our founder spent more than twelve years in technology at some of the largest companies in the world, running partnerships with the likes of IBM, BT and Vodafone. That work was about getting big organisations to actually adopt something, which turns out to be most of the job here too.

We then built SurgeLab: an AI platform that measures how ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude describe a business, fixes what they get wrong, and runs that business's marketing every week. It is a working product with paying customers, not a slide deck.

The consultancy is the same thinking applied directly to your company instead of through a product. It is deliberately a small number of engagements at a time.

Before you book

Questions

What kind of companies do you work with?

Established businesses that hold a lot of information and want AI working inside it rather than alongside it. So far that has meant family offices and recruitment, plus the brands the SurgeLab platform runs.

Do we have to replace our existing systems?

No. The work is a layer on top of the CRM, database and documents you already have, so your systems stay where they are and your team keeps working the way it works.

Is this the same as the SurgeLab product?

No. SurgeLab is a subscription platform that runs marketing for small and mid-sized businesses. The consultancy is us working directly with your company, on your systems, and is a separate engagement.

How does an engagement start?

A thirty minute call, then one scoped piece of work priced before it starts. If that first piece pays for itself there is usually an obvious next one, and if it does not, it is a clean place to stop.
