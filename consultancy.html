What this is

Three ways in

An intelligent layer over your systems

You already have a CRM, a database, a pile of documents and a way of working. Rather than replace any of it, we put a layer on top that can read it, answer questions about it and do the repetitive work inside it. Your systems stay where they are.

Getting more from the AI you pay for

Most companies are already paying for AI and using a fraction of it. We look at what your teams actually do all day, find the work worth handing over, and set it up properly. Then we measure whether it saved anything.

Training that starts at the top

Enablement for the people who set direction, not a lunchtime webinar. Your leadership team, working in your own tools, on your own problems, until using AI well is normal rather than a project.

Who this suits

Where it has worked

The pattern is the same in every case: a business with good people, a lot of accumulated information, and no obvious way to get AI into the middle of it without breaking what already works.

Family offices

Years of documents, correspondence and holdings sitting in places nobody can search properly. The work is making all of it answerable, without any of it leaving where it should be.

Recruitment

A database full of people and roles that only pays off if someone remembers who is in it. The work is matching, drafting and follow-up done in the background, with a human making every decision that counts.

SurgeLab itself

The clearest example. A team of 25 AI agents with a Chief of Staff keeping them honest, running marketing for real businesses every week. Built from scratch, and the reason any of this advice is worth taking.

Who you are dealing with

Twelve years in tech,
then this

James spent more than twelve years in technology at some of the largest companies in the world, running partnerships with the likes of IBM, BT and Vodafone. That work was about getting big organisations to actually adopt something, which turns out to be most of the job here too.

He then built SurgeLab: an AI platform that measures how ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude describe a business, fixes what they get wrong, and runs that business's marketing every week. It is a working product with paying customers, not a slide deck.

The consultancy is the same thinking applied directly to your company instead of through a product. It is deliberately a small number of engagements at a time.
