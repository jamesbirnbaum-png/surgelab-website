Partner levels

The more brands, the better your rate

Three levels. Each one drops your wholesale cost per brand. You choose what to charge on top, so every brand you add is worth more than the last. The exact numbers come on the call, never on a public page.

LEVEL 1

1 to 4 brands

Partner rate from your first brand. White-label available as an add-on.

LEVEL 2

5 to 9 brands

A lower rate on every brand, not just the new ones. Credits pool across all of them.

LEVEL 3

10 or more

The lowest rate we offer. Your own dashboard for the whole roster, and a direct line to us.
