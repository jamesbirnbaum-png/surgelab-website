Privacy policy

Effective 22 July 2026

Who we are

SurgeLab ("we", "us") provides a done-for-you social media content service for small and mid-sized businesses and agencies. We generate branded social content on behalf of our clients and schedule it to their connected accounts. This policy explains what data we handle and why.

For any privacy question, or to request deletion of your data, contact support@surgelab.co.

What we collect

Account data. Email address and authentication identifiers for the operators and collaborators who log in to the platform.

Client brand data. Business details, website content, uploaded photos and logos, product information, and tone-of-voice preferences supplied by or on behalf of a client so we can generate content for them.

Connected platform data. Where a client connects a third-party account (such as Google Business Profile or a scheduling tool), we receive the data that connection provides. This is described in more detail below.

Usage data. Records of content generated, scheduled, and published, plus the compute cost of each generation, so we can enforce per-client spending limits.

Enquiry data. If you use the form on our agencies page, we receive the agency name, email address and brand count you enter, so we can reply. Those submissions are stored by Netlify, which hosts this website.

We do not collect payment card details through this platform. Payments are taken by Stripe on its own secure page.

How we use Google user data

Where a client connects their Google Business Profile, we request the business.manage scope. We use that access only to:

  • read the business locations the account manages, so the client can pick one;
  • read reviews for that location, so the client can see and reply to them;
  • publish posts to that location when the client approves them.

Limited Use. SurgeLab's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to train generalised artificial intelligence models.

A client can disconnect at any time from within the platform, or by revoking access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. On disconnection we delete the stored access and refresh tokens.

Service providers

We use third-party processors to run the service. Each receives only the data needed for its function: Supabase (database and authentication), Vercel (hosting), Anthropic and OpenAI (text generation), FAL.ai (image and video generation), ElevenLabs (voice), Shotstack (video assembly), Firecrawl (website scanning), Metricool (scheduling to social networks), Google (Business Profile and Places), Resend (transactional email), Stripe (payments) and Netlify (hosting of this website and its enquiry form).

Content we send to AI providers is sent for the purpose of generating that client's content only. We do not sell personal data to anyone.

Retention and security

We keep client brand data and generated content for as long as the client account is active, and delete it on request. Access tokens are stored encrypted at rest and are deleted when a connection is removed. Access to client data within the platform is restricted per project, and administrative data is restricted to SurgeLab staff.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete the personal data we hold about you, and to object to certain processing. Email support@surgelab.co and we will respond within 30 days.

Cookies

This website sets no cookies. Inside the platform we use only the cookies and local storage needed to keep you signed in and remember which brand you were last working on. We do not run advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Children

The service is for business use and is not directed at anyone under 18.

Changes

If we make a material change to this policy we will update the effective date above and, where we hold an email address for you, let you know.
